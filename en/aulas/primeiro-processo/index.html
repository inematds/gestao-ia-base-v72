Lesson 8 · 20 min

Your first diagnosis + What changed

The best first step in the program is a process in your company, not a tool. The project for this course is the sheet for the first process you will manage with agents: owner, volume, today's measure and the course that answers your biggest question. At the end, what changed in the market this quarter, with date and source.

Start the lesson

  1. 1Choose the first process using three filters, not the trendy tool
  2. 2Measure how the process stands today, before any agent touches it
  3. 3Link your biggest question to the course in the program that answers it
  4. 4What changed through October 2026, with date and source

Goal: choose the first process to manage with agents, with owner, volume and today's measure, and the next course in the program.

02 · From candidate to sheet

Four steps. Horizonte (a fictional study company) does the practice run; your process is the one that counts.

1. Process, not tool

Whoever starts with the tool ends up looking for a process that fits it. Whoever starts with the process first asks whether it should still exist this way, and only then chooses a person, an agent or automation.

Pick just one. The first process is for learning to manage; the second comes out faster.

At HorizonteCandidates: the order from WhatsApp through to billing, collections (Carlos decides in November whether Hora takes it over), and the daily customer service summary.

2. Three filters

Volume: it happens every week, in an amount worth looking at. Rule: someone can write down what is right and wrong. Damage: you know what can go wrong and how big the mess would be.

A process that fails the third filter is not forbidden; it just isn't the first one. Start where the error is known and can be contained.

At HorizonteThe order passes all three: 141 to 171 orders per week on the dashboard, a written discount policy and known damage (discount, large order). Collections has no written rule yet.

3. Today's measure

Choose two or three measures the process already lets you count: how many cases, how many needed correction, how much time from start to finish. Measure for two weeks, as it is, without changing anything.

Without this starting point, any number afterward becomes an impression. It is failure F11 from the lab: the dashboard started on the day Hora was turned on.

At HorizonteMeasure for two weeks: orders accepted without correction at picking, hours from order to invoicing, and exceptions per day. Owner: Patrícia.

4. The next course

Write your biggest question about the process in one sentence. It picks the course: G2 if it is "should it exist this way?", G3 if it is "how do I hand it to the agent and how far does it go?", G4 if it is model and cost, G1 if it is the people, G5 if it is governing and measuring.

The program's suggested order (G0, G2, G3, G4, G1, G5) applies when there is no urgency. A present risk beats the order.

At HorizontePatrícia's biggest question is "how far can Hora go on its own?". Next course: G3. Then G2, to redesign the order.

04 · What changed · October 2026

Quarterly dashboard, updated with each course review. Each item has a date, a source and a type of claim, plus a consequence for how you choose your first process.

1. Tools change in months

OpenAI: Workspace Agents, which replace Custom GPTs, moved to per-token billing (07/06/2026), and the AgentKit Agent Builder will be shut down on 11/30/2026. Microsoft: Agent 365, announced at Build (06/2026).

Sources: each vendor's release notes and announcements. These are swappable examples: next quarter the list will be different.

For your first processChoose the process and the measure before the tool. If the tool changes, the process and the starting point still hold.

2. Few agents in real use

Gartner (2026, Hype Cycle for agentic AI): only 17% of companies have agents in use, worldwide. GFT, Tech Trends 2026 LATAM with BCG data: 18% of Brazilian companies integrate agents, versus 13% worldwide.

Different definitions, same reading: if you are just starting now, you are not behind most others.

For your first processOne well-chosen, well-measured process is worth more than five agents switched on with no owner.

3. Governance behind adoption

Okta, AI Agents at Work 2026 (vendor survey): almost two thirds of organizations apply weaker controls to agents than to people. Deloitte (09/17/2026): 27% of Brazilian companies have mature governance.

Gartner (02/17/2026, forecast): spending on AI governance rises from US$ 492 million in 2026 to more than US$ 1 billion in 2030.

For your first processOwner, permissions and logging go into your first process from day one, not after the first scare.

4. Management at the center

Microsoft, Work Trend Index 2026 (05/2026, vendor survey): when the manager uses AI in front of the team, perceived value goes up 17 points. IT Forum (2026, opinion column): the year companies stop building agents and start operating them.

Next review of this dashboard: January 2027. What is method (lessons 1 to 7) does not change; what is product and numbers does.

For your first processThe person who leads the first process is the manager of the area, in front of the team. Don't outsource the example.
