Lesson 7 · 20 min

Career: who is adjusting and how

When the agent handles the information handoff and the report, the work of whoever coordinates changes shape. Managers, juniors, HR and IT are adjusting in different ways, and the market already offers governance, security and vendor certifications. In this lesson you choose your own move, learn how to evaluate a certification, and leave with a 90-day plan.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The four moves: manager, junior, HR and IT
  2. 2What AIGP, AAISM, ISO/IEC 42001 and vendor credentials cover
  3. 3Why none of them teaches you to manage an agent day to day
  4. 4A 90-day plan with dated milestones and one certification evaluated

Goal: choose your career move and evaluate an external certification by what it covers and requires, before deciding to take it.

02 · What each move asks of you

Four readings, with the source for each fact. The examples are from Horizonte, a fictional study company.

1. Manager

Gartner's October 2024 forecast speaks of cutting layers of middle management by the end of 2026. Deloitte and McKinsey describe the same manager differently: less handoff, more coordination, review and training.

The move is to stop being the channel that information passes through and become the person who sets limits, reviews exceptions and develops the team, both people and agents.

At HorizontePatrícia stops building the weekly report by hand and starts deciding the 5% to 10% discounts, approving orders above R$ 5 mil and developing Juliana.

2. Junior

The Stanford Digital Economy Lab (08/2026) recorded a relative drop of about 19% in employment for young people aged 22 to 25 in the occupations most exposed to AI, without claiming that AI is the cause.

Whoever comes in starts reviewing what the agent did. A manager who wants to develop people has to make sure the junior also does the work, not just checks it.

At HorizonteToday Juliana answers what Hora doesn't know. To grow, she needs to decide cases, not just pass them along.

3. HR

Forrester (Predictions 2026) forecasts that HR platforms will include management of the "digital employee." It is a forecast, not a released product.

For people in HR, the move starts before the tool: onboard the agent, say who evaluates it, and what happens to the work of the people who used to handle that task.

At HorizonteThere is no dedicated HR. Today nobody has recorded that Hora "joined" the sales team, or who evaluates it.

4. IT

Okta, AI Agents at Work 2026: almost two thirds of organizations apply weaker security controls to agents than to employees. Microsoft announced Agent 365 at Build (06/2026), aimed at managing agents in the company.

The move for IT is to treat each agent as an identity: with its own credential, minimum permissions and a record of what it did.

At HorizonteThe email assistant uses the shared commercial@ account with permission to read, send and delete, and Hora keeps no record of its actions. Neither of them has its own identity.

03 · The certification landscape and how to evaluate one

What existed in October 2026, according to the training program's market overview. No independent "agent management" certification was found. Before deciding, check the price and rules on the official page.

Sheet · evaluate a certification before taking it
EVALUATE CERTIFICATION · <name> · <issuer> · consulted on <date>

1. WHAT IT COVERS (official page): <governance / security / risk / management system / building agents>
2. PREREQUISITE: <none / another certification / experience>
3. IS IT FOR THE PERSON OR THE COMPANY? <...>
4. DOES MY ROLE OR MY CLIENT REQUIRE IT? [ ] yes, where: <tender, questionnaire, job posting>  [ ] no
5. DOES IT TEACH HOW TO MANAGE AN AGENT DAY TO DAY? [ ] yes [ ] no, it covers: <...>
6. COST AND TIME (official page): <...>

DECISION: [ ] take it by <deadline>  [ ] not now, because <reason>  [ ] never, because <reason>

The INEMA certificate is a qualification, not a regulated professional certification, and it doesn't replace AIGP or ISO/IEC 42001 when the role or the client requires one of them.

  1. All price and timing information comes from the official page, with the date you consulted it.
  2. Line 4 of the sheet cites where the requirement appears (job posting, tender, client questionnaire) or says "no."
  3. The decision has a deadline or a reason.
  4. Common mistake: choosing by the fame of the acronym. A governance certification doesn't teach you how to delegate to an agent, and a vendor credential teaches you to build in that vendor's tool.

04 · Template to copy: 90-day plan

One page. Three milestones, one per month, and one certification evaluated, even if the decision is not to take it now.

My career's 90-day plan
90-DAY PLAN · <name> · start <date>

TODAY: <role> · MOVE: <manager → hybrid manager / junior → supervisor / HR → people and agents / IT → identity and permission / other>
WHAT LEAVES MY WORK: <...>
WHAT STAYS AND GROWS: <...>

MILESTONE 1 (by <date>): <deliverable you can show, e.g.: map of the 9 objects of the area approved>
MILESTONE 2 (by <date>): <e.g.: first process with a measured baseline>
MILESTONE 3 (by <date>): <e.g.: one agent with an owner, authority limit and weekly review>

PROGRAM COURSES: <G2, G3...> in the order <...>
EXTERNAL CERTIFICATION EVALUATED: <name> · decision: <take it by X / not now because Y>
WHO FOLLOWS UP: <name> · conversation on <date>
Simulation: illustrative example

Patrícia Lima · Horizonte Distribuidora, fictional study company · start 11/03/2026

  • Move: manager → hybrid team manager.
  • Leaves: weekly report built by hand. Grows: authority limit, exception queue, developing Juliana.
  • Milestone 1 (11/30): map of the 9 objects of the sales area with 9 owners.
  • Milestone 2 (12/31): two weeks of order baseline measured.
  • Milestone 3 (01/31): Hora's hiring dossier approved by Carlos (course G3).
  • Certification evaluated: AIGP. Decision: not now; no Horizonte client asks for it, and it covers governance, not the day-to-day routine with Hora.
  • Follows up: Carlos, conversation at the end of each month.

05 · Practice

Three Horizonte people to develop; your own career for real.

1 · Three moves at Horizonte

20 min

For Patrícia, Juliana and Rafael, write each person's move (from the four in the diagram), what leaves and what grows in their work. Then, for each one, say whether any certification in the overview would make sense and why, using the evaluation sheet.

Done when: all three have a move, one line for what leaves and one for what grows, and a decision about certification with a reason ("not needed" also counts, as long as it says why).

2 · Your 90-day plan

30 min

Fill in the 90-day plan. Choose one certification from the overview that has some connection to your role, open the official page, run the prompt on its text and fill in the evaluation sheet. Schedule the first conversation with whoever will follow up on the plan.

Done when: the plan has the move, three dated milestones, the program courses in the chosen order, one external certification evaluated with the sheet with a decision and reason, and the first conversation scheduled on the calendar.
