Lesson 2 · 17 min

Managing an agent looks like managing people

What a company did with a new employee, it needs to do with each agent: give the role, the goal, the tools, the limits and the evaluation. The words change, not the logic. In this lesson you build the translation of a job into an agent, see the two items that people did not have, and learn where the comparison stops.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The six pairs that carry people management over to agent management
  2. 2The two items that people did not have: explicit context and graduated autonomy
  3. 3Whoever posts does not approve: the agent prepares, the person decides
  4. 4Where the comparison stops, and the mistake of treating people like machines

Goal: translate a person's job, responsibility, tools, limits and evaluation into an agent, and say where the comparison stops.

02 · The same logic in four situations

Three professions where the rule already existed for people, and the point where the comparison stops. The Horizonte examples come from the fictional study company.

1. The same work

Think about what a good manager knows about the team: what the goal is, who does what, what information each person needs, how much autonomy they have, how to measure, and when to step in. Now swap "person" for "agent". It's practically the same list.

Microsoft gave this a name in the 2025 Work Trend Index: the agent boss, a human manager of one or more agents, and the human-agent ratio, the balance between human oversight and agent efficiency. It's the vendor's framing, useful as vocabulary.

At HorizontePatrícia became Hora's boss without anyone telling her. She already knew how to manage Juliana; she just hadn't written down for Hora what she would say to a new saleswoman.

2. Whoever posts does not approve

In accounting, separation of duties, authority limits and human review have always applied to the assistant. They apply the same way to the agent: it goes up to the door of the signature, never past it.

This isn't distrust of the agent. It's the same rule that protects the company from any error, human or not.

At HorizonteHora records the order. An order above R$ 5 thousand only goes to picking with Patrícia's approval (fictional study policy).

3. Prepares, doesn't decide

In legal work, the agent does the first read and the lawyer decides and signs: responsibility isn't delegated, it's organized. In health care, the administrative side can go to AI, but some decisions stay outside the agent's authority limit.

In both cases the limit is a management decision, made before turning the agent on, not after the first scare.

At HorizonteHora gives a discount of up to 5% on its own to a registered customer. From 5% to 10%, it prepares and Patrícia decides. Above that, it doesn't even offer.

4. Where the comparison stops

A person has needs, rights and relationships; you talk with them, and you look after their workload and their future. An agent is managed by performance, reliability and security. With an agent, you want to know how many deliveries came out right.

Error changes too: with a person, it becomes a conversation and learning; with an agent, it becomes a corrected instruction and a new test case. The serious mistake is mixing the two up.

At HorizonteSince Hora arrived, Juliana answers only what Hora doesn't know. Patrícia measures Hora by accuracy; with Juliana, she talks about what changed in her work.

03 · Template to copy: the before × now table

One page per role. Start with what you would tell a new person in that role and translate it line by line.

Before × now table
BEFORE × NOW TABLE · role: <e.g., salesperson who takes orders> · agent: <name> · <date>

ITEM          | FOR THE PERSON (before)          | FOR THE AGENT (now)                | HOW I CHECK
Role          | <job title>                      | <process + result, 1 sentence>     | <does an outsider understand?>
Goal          | <responsibility>                 | <result you can check>             | <where it is recorded>
Context       | (learned by being around)        | <what it gets in writing>          | <who keeps it up to date>
Tools         | <systems, phone, spreadsheet>    | <look up / create / send ...>      | <which one can change data?>
Autonomy      | <agreed limit>                   | <does alone / prepares / never>    | <ceiling as a number>
Evaluation    | <performance review>             | <indicator + target + when I look> | <this week's number>

PERSON RESPONSIBLE FOR THE AGENT'S RESULT: <name, from the business>
WHAT STAYS WITH THE PERSON (and why): <decision, relationship, exception ...>
WHERE THE COMPARISON STOPS: <what I look after in the person and not in the agent>
Prompt · review the table

Simulation: illustrative example

Replenishment saleswoman → Hora · Horizonte Distribuidora, fictional study company

  1. Each agent line can be checked by someone from another area.
  2. The context line has at least one thing the person knew without it being written down.
  3. The autonomy has a number where there is value (discount, order amount).
  4. Common mistake: copying the person's job description to the agent. The agent doesn't understand "common sense"; it understands rules and examples.

04 · Practice

At Horizonte, to practice. Then with a role from your team.

1 · Hora's table

20 min

Read Hora's prompt and configuration in the lab. Copy the template and fill in the before × now table, taking a replenishment saleswoman as the "before". Use this lesson's fictional policy (5%, 10%, R$ 5 thousand). Only then compare with the example.

Done when: all six lines are filled in, each line in the agent column has something another person can check (a record, a number or a rule), the autonomy cites the three numbers of the policy, and the responsible party is Patrícia.

2 · A role on your team

25 min

Pick a repetitive task that a person on your team does today and that an agent could take over in part. Fill in the table, run the review prompt and write the two final lines: what stays with the person and where the comparison stops.

Done when: the table has all six rows, a named owner, at least one thing that stays with the person along with the reason, and one sentence saying what you look after in the person and not in the agent.

Lab piece: Horizonte's WhatsApp agent (prompt, configuration and conversations; fictional study company).

06 · Summary

Same logicRole, goal, tools, autonomy and evaluation, just like with people.
Two new itemsExplicit context and graduated autonomy, written down.
Prepares, doesn't decideThe agent goes up to the signature gate; whoever posts does not approve.
Where it stopsA person has workload and a future; an agent has accuracy, time and cost.
